# Copy Studio

Copy Studio is a native dashboard tab under **Workspace**, beside Create. It includes Direct to Call, Lead Magnet, and Video Sales Letter templates; brief entry and source extraction; strategy and draft generation; claim checks; section editing and AI rewriting; undo; preview; ChatGPT landing-page design prompts; copy export; and project backup/import. Custom copy-generation prompts and AI Consultation are not included.

The dashboard owns its UI and API. The original `copy_generator` project and its server are not dependencies. The new stylesheet is scoped to `.copy-studio` and uses the dashboard's existing color and font tokens. The tab loads on first use and stays mounted when changing dashboard screens, so a running generation can finish without losing the draft.

## Client onboarding and offer focus

The brief starts with **Prefill from page** and **Prefill from onboarding**. Page prefill reads the source, asks which offer to use when needed, and proposes supported fields for review; applying them also retains the complete captured copy as the voice/context reference. **Add as reference only** remains a secondary action for keeping an already reviewed brief unchanged. Onboarding prefill reads the client's saved answers and lists missing essentials rather than inventing answers or marking an incomplete client form complete.

Extraction accepts up to 128 model citations because one fact can have multiple supporting passages. After checking the original wording, the server builds a 24-item review index with a citation for each populated field before supplemental citations. Valid source-rich responses no longer fail solely for exceeding the UI's citation count. Inline attributed quotations can also be detected outside dedicated testimonial routes when they explicitly reference the provider named in the captured homepage title; unrelated template endorsements remain excluded. Full quotations remain in the proof archive independently of the compact review index.

Both paths use the same five essentials as client onboarding: Offer name; What are you offering?; Who is it for?; What problem are they facing?; What outcome do they want? These labels share one definition. Onboarding requires all five answers (3–6,000 characters each) for completion; draft saves remain possible. Older combined offer answers retain their short first line as the editable name only when the new name field did not exist.

Choose **client onboarding** inside Copy Studio, search by client, project, or page URL, then review the answers to apply. Website matches are suggestions; multiple projects on the same website remain selectable. The studio reads only the client's offer, audience, problem, outcome, traffic/qualification context, and must-keep/avoid notes. Agency fees, agency deliverables, portal tokens, signatures, access data, and consent records are excluded from the response. No onboarding record is edited by Copy Studio.

Choose the onboarding/current offer, one specific offer, or a new offer. Each option keeps a separate brief within the copy project. Switching options starts a clean brief, and switching back restores previous edits. Creative direction and client notes are included in strategy, generation, and section rewrites. Changes to them mark an existing draft as outdated.

Page import is optional. It detects distinct offers and requires a choice when several offers are found. Extraction for a chosen offer uses only that offer's facts, backed by source quotes. Importing a different named offer starts a separate copy project with the reviewed fields and retains the original project. Unsupported or ambiguous offers are not silently substituted.

An unreadable or incomplete AI-client response receives one automatic retry within the original AI timeout. Selecting an offer reuses the captured website; recovery does not restart its crawl or apply unreviewed answers. Authentication, quota, gateway restrictions and cancelled requests are not retried by this recovery step. Repeated invalid output still fails safely without replacing the existing draft.

The selected onboarding's identity and revision are saved in browser storage and project backups. Opening the picker refreshes available answers; newer revisions are flagged for review. Existing version-2 backups remain supported.

## Direct to Call sections and headings

The Direct to Call outline now contains hero, problem, approach, call details, audience fit, **About me**, proof, objections, and the final CTA, in that order. Add the coach's approved name, relevant background, experience and qualifications in **Give the copy substance → Coach background & credentials**. Optional source extraction can populate that field for review using exact source quotes. Missing background leaves the public About me section empty and requests details in the writer's notes; the generator does not fabricate credentials or a personal story. Supplied background requires a populated introduction. The design prompt includes a distinct biography layout and an approved portrait when available.

Supporting headings now introduce their section clearly, for example “How I help,” “What the conversation covers,” “Who this is for,” and “About me.” They can be specific to the client, while the hero remains outcome-led and the final CTA action-led. These rules apply to generation and section rewrites. Existing eight-section drafts and backups remain readable/exportable; regenerate them to adopt the new outline and heading instructions.

## Original copy as a full reference

Generation combines the reviewed brief and onboarding answers, the full captured original copy, and the operator's creative direction. The original copy is supplied to strategy, drafting, claim repair, and section rewrites. It guides voice, vocabulary, rhythm, context, positioning and useful material. The approved brief controls factual claims, and requested changes guide the rewrite within those facts. Conflicting or outdated source details and other offers do not override the selected offer.

Choose **Start with an existing page → Use full copy as reference** to attach a URL or pasted copy while keeping every brief field unchanged. Reading and attaching the reference does not require an AI extraction request. **Extract offer facts** remains available when you want to review additional facts for the brief. Existing imported pages automatically become full references for subsequent generation.

The reference panel shows the captured text and lets you preserve the client's voice (default), rework the voice while retaining context, or turn the reference off. A specific voice instruction takes priority over the original voice; the generic default voice does not. URL import now recursively follows public same-origin links, including navigation and footer links, and discovers routes through sitemap indexes and robots-declared sitemaps. It starts with the supplied page and includes the homepage even when the supplied page has no link to it. About, testimonial, result and service routes become one reference with source-page URL markers. Other offers still require explicit selection; provider credentials and offer-specific proof remain subject to review.

Website coverage lists pages read, duplicates, unreadable/failed routes, pending routes and shortened text. Crawls are bounded at 100 page attempts, 2,000 discovered routes, 20 sitemap documents and two minutes, with three concurrent requests and per-request deadlines. Only public HTML is read; unlisted routes absent from links/sitemaps, JavaScript-only content, embedded content and login-protected pages cannot be guaranteed. External links, access/action routes and non-page assets are excluded. Every connection and redirect retains public-address validation and DNS pinning; discovered routes cannot redirect outside the final seed origin. The reference limit is now 240,000 characters, shared across pages so a long homepage cannot consume the entire budget, with a 24,000-character per-page ceiling. Limits and missing content are reported rather than presented as full coverage. The full retained reference passes through offer selection, strategy, generation and rewrites. For missing content, paste the visible copy. Existing saved references are not recrawled automatically; use Change to import the site again. The reference and coverage persist in project backups and browser storage. Changing the text or reference setting marks an existing draft as outdated.

## Landing page design prompt

After generating copy, open **Design prompt** beside Sections and Preview. It automatically combines the latest saved draft with its offer, audience, conversion goal, section-specific layout guidance, and design exclusions. **Copy design prompt** prepares it for ChatGPT; **Download prompt** saves a text file. There is no second AI request. Saved edits, section rewrites, undo, and project reloads all produce the matching prompt. When the brief changes, the prompt keeps the existing draft's generation snapshot until a new draft is generated.

When the generated draft includes an original-page reference, the design handoff also supplies its canonical public marketing-page URL for client asset research. Query strings and fragments are removed; credential-bearing URLs, private/literal hosts, nonstandard ports, common access paths and non-web URLs are excluded. The URL follows the draft's generation snapshot, so a new ungenerated reference cannot replace the saved draft's client. Raw source copy and private onboarding metadata remain excluded. The builder must verify the actual provider's identity before choosing a portrait and cannot import other offers or claims. The old page supplies identity and asset candidates, not the design quality target.

The prompt carries a shared quality standard in `shared/design-direction.ts`, with a distinct visual identity derived for each offer. Every handoff now requires a continuous dark canvas with readable light text. Offer-specific deep neutral or chromatic grounds, accents, typography, composition and atmosphere still vary; there is no shared palette or starter stylesheet. The direction is self-contained, rich contemporary art direction: custom atmosphere, visible depth, expressive typography and a clear conversion path. Framer, Dribbble, Linear, Vercel and Stripe describe a level of craft, not pages to copy. No reference page or screenshot is required. Client source URLs are for authentic asset discovery only; their layout and background composition must not be reproduced. Palette, type, geometry and atmospheric treatment vary for each offer.

Before coding, the builder interprets the audience, voice, message and conversion decision, considers three materially different directions, then selects and documents its strongest offer-specific identity internally. It defines its own actual palette, typography, hero geometry, background language, section rhythm and action treatment. A counterfactual check rejects choices that could be transferred unchanged to an unrelated offer. Where previous pages are available, the builder compares visual signatures and avoids unintended repetition. Different colors on an identical composition do not count as distinct designs.

The canvas remains coherent through the page, with transparent content sections and no alternating colored bands, tinted conversation panels or separate closing boxes. Offer-specific deep neutral and dark chromatic grounds are valid. Sculpted light, custom grain-treated meshes, noise, spatial geometry, dimensional abstract artwork and layered authentic imagery are creative options; dots and overhead lighting remain optional. No technique is compulsory. The builder must compose an original atmospheric idea with deliberate placement, transitions, grain scale and a relationship to the type, keeping reading areas calm. A flat fill or almost invisible effect fails the quality standard. There is no mandatory hero alignment, font family, colored-word treatment or closing layout. Supporting sections share a responsive wider column grid with comfortable paragraph measures, while their compositions follow their actual content roles. Reading width and whole-section width are separate decisions. A 12-column frame around 1240–1360px at a 1440px viewport and 50–65-character paragraphs are adaptable starting points. Featured statements, open topic groups, compact checklists, portrait/biography, featured evidence and closing actions may use different grid spans, density and scale. Consistent anchors and gutters preserve coherence without forcing a repeated article layout. Short sections avoid excessive gaps. There is no layout-variation quota: arbitrary zigzags, remote title/body columns, disconnected lists and empty half-pages remain excluded. Color roles distinguish clear essential copy, display text, accents, actions and quieter metadata; all copy must not inherit the same muted accent. Suggested wide-desktop type ranges define distinct roles and adapt to the actual copy and viewport. Complete supplied testimonials, labels and attributions remain visible without excerpting. Navigation, repeated card grids, stock gradient blobs, unmodified preset washes, fabricated proof and competing actions remain excluded. Custom grain-treated mesh gradients are explicitly allowed. Final copy, descriptive headings, paragraph breaks and section order remain exact; empty sections stay omitted and editorial notes stay private.

When asked for a new design direction, the builder retains the same visual richness, depth, hierarchy and continuous dark environment while rethinking its creative expression. Exact copy, section order, approved assets, functionality and real integrations remain intact. The separate handoff describes the implemented palette, typography, composition and atmosphere so follow-up feedback can be specific.

The builder must inspect actual desktop/mobile renders and check offer-specific identity, visible controlled depth, hierarchy, reading comfort, content-specific section compositions and a full-page silhouette beyond repeated heading/paragraph blocks, exact copy and functioning supplied interactions. Batch review compares first viewports and full-page silhouettes to catch template reuse. Named failures require revision and another capture. It must distinguish code checks from visual verification when rendering is unavailable. Missing assets and booking/form destinations must be supplied separately. The prompt guides the external builder; Copy Studio itself does not render or validate that external landing page or claim improved conversion rates.

## Configuration

The existing working Copy Studio provider settings were copied into the ignored `.env.local` and `.env.docker.local` files. Other AI features retain their original credentials. Compose already loads the Docker override for the dashboard service. No credentials are sent to the browser or included in project exports.

The optional variables are:

```dotenv
COPY_STUDIO_API_KEY=
COPY_STUDIO_BASE_URL=https://agentrouter.org/v1
COPY_STUDIO_MODEL=deepseek-v4-flash
COPY_STUDIO_TIMEOUT_MS=90000
```

Without a dedicated key, endpoint, or model, the corresponding `AGENTROUTER_*` setting is used. Use **Tools → Setup & storage → Test AI connection** to verify a real provider request. Restart the dashboard after environment changes.

AgentRouter uses the same pinned, genuine Qwen Code client as the working standalone tool. Tools, customizations, telemetry, and chat recording are disabled. The private temporary relay handles AgentRouter's null and billing stream frames while preserving real completion and error events. The CLI is resolved from the dashboard's own installation and included in Next.js standalone output; Docker also copies the full dependency tree.

## Saved projects

Projects use this browser's local storage at the dashboard address, under `compel:copy-studio:library:v2`. They are separate from lead and content databases. Saved project data from `localhost:3874` is not automatically accessible at `localhost:3000` because browser storage is scoped by origin. To bring an existing project over, export **Project backup** from the standalone tool, then choose **Tools → Import a project** in the dashboard. The file format is unchanged.

## Implementation

- `src/components/copy-studio/`: integrated workspace, review dialogs, persistence, and scoped styles.
- `src/lib/copy-studio/shared/`: schemas, templates, and deterministic copy checks; imports `zod/v4` without changing the dashboard's Zod 3 callers.
- `src/lib/copy-studio/server/`: generation, provider transport, safe URL extraction, and HTTP adapter.
- `src/app/api/copy-studio/[operation]/route.ts`: Node-runtime routes for onboarding selection, health, connection, generation, revision, import, and reading references.

The API enforces same-origin JSON requests, bounded request sizes, rate and concurrency limits, and a five-minute deadline. Response cancellation stops the active provider call. Extracted facts require source quotes and are applied only after review. A failed request preserves the previous completed draft. Automated claim checks are not exhaustive fact verification.

## Large source requests

Captured website text remains attached in full within the existing 240,000-character crawl budget. The server searches all captured text locally and selects exact passages with route attribution when a capture exceeds the AI input allowance. Offer relevance, About/credentials routes, and testimonials/results routes influence selection; this is retrieval, not a claim that the model read every captured word. The prefill review discloses excerpt use, and the existing coverage view still reports unread routes and shortened pages. The model cites supplied passage IDs; the server copies the original passage text into evidence and verifies it against the retained capture. Unknown passage IDs cannot approve fields or offers. This prevents rewritten model quotes from silently discarding supported essentials or provider credentials. Approved brief facts are never shortened to fit a request.

Strategy, drafting, claim repair, and section rewrites each budget source context against their own instructions and draft. Groq defaults to a conservative 20,000-byte input allowance; other endpoints default to 80,000 bytes. COPY_STUDIO_INPUT_BYTES optionally sets an allowance between 8,000 and 200,000 bytes; actual provider token limits still apply. A 413 triggers at most one rebuild with a smaller source allowance rather than resending the unchanged request. Short 429 cooldowns honor Retry-After with cancellation, at most two retries and 60 seconds total wait per call; longer or unspecified limits return an actionable error.


## Evidence, frameworks and public headings

The design handoff now requires a near-black base (relative luminance at most 0.01), opaque near-white heading and light body tokens, a 7:1 contrast target for essential reading text, and verification against the composited light/mesh and marker backgrounds. It explicitly plans selective underline/marker/solid emphasis on exact important phrases and responsive section gaps (112–160px desktop and 64–88px mobile starting ranges, adjusted for actual content). These are quality constraints, not a shared accent palette or hero template.

Full social proof is exempt from ordinary brief/body length limits. Extraction version 3 removes the eight-account and 3,000-character quote cutoffs, preserves multi-paragraph testimony and author-first/author-last layouts, and prevents training labels from being merged into another client's account. Quote-then-author sequences retain that pairing for every speaker, including third-person and As-my openings. Testimonial routes and ordinary pages containing a testimonial section are retained before other pages share the capture budget. The crawl still reports unread/truncated routes, including any proof-bearing page that exceeds the overall capture budget; it cannot establish that inaccessible or unlisted material was captured.

The model's testimonial choices recommend prominence rather than deleting other detected accounts. Every detected source-attributed account is retained for operator review, with categories distinguishing client experiences, professional recommendations and training feedback. Approval remains required; feedback for a different service must not be represented as results from the selected offer. Complete approved quotes and attributions are inserted by the server after generation and rewrites. Old excerpt/count requests no longer remove archive entries. Large archives send only whole accounts that fit the model request, with an explicit archive notice; the stored originals and final copy remain complete. The ordinary 6,000-character limits still apply to other fields and sections. An archive exceeding the larger storage limit raises an error instead of silently dropping proof.

Copy Studio's section and preview views feature three accounts and use a native Show all testimonials disclosure for the rest. All full accounts are already in the document. Copy/Markdown exports and the design prompt retain the entire archive. The designer is instructed to feature the strongest 2–3 relevant accounts, retain all other approved testimony in the same section under an accessible inline disclosure, preserve categories, and verify the rendered count. No carousels, quote excerpts, line clamps or fetch-on-expand. Existing projects need a fresh page prefill and regeneration to recover material absent from older captures or abbreviated drafts.

Prefill keeps source-attributed testimonials separate from numerical proof. Dedicated testimonial/review routes are searched for adjacent author-and-feedback pairs before general text is shortened. The model recommends relevant testimonial IDs for prominence; the server retains the complete detected archive with original feedback, author and route. Source-attributed testimonials are rendered from approved source data after drafting, claim repair and proof rewrites. Complete quotations retain their original words and attribution; a model paraphrase cannot replace them. Rejecting an unrelated statistic leaves approved testimonials intact, including personal endorsements that contain numbers. Indexed feedback leaves room for core homepage offer, outcome and next-step passages. Populated essentials with unverifiable citations are rechecked once; genuinely unsupported answers remain omitted with a warning. Existing captures from the older extraction version open for a fresh read instead of reusing stale missing-proof results. Approval remains required.

Recommended frameworks now resolve by page goal: PAS for Direct to Call, AIDA for Lead Magnet and VSL without supplied proof, and 4 Ps for VSL with proof. Explicit selections take priority. Strategy generation must produce an offer-specific plan for every named stage in order and pass factual claim checks before drafting; that plan guides drafting, repairs and rewrites. The saved strategy records the actual framework, recommendation reason and section mapping. Earlier saved drafts remain readable and are labelled when they have no recorded framework.

Strategy requests explicitly state field limits, including the 600-character maximum for each stage application. Internal references to approved evidence IDs and instructions to omit price or policy are excluded from the strategy's public-claim scan. Affirmative planned claims remain checked, and public copy keeps the full claim checks. This prevents valid planning text from consuming the one structural retry and blocking page generation.

The page framework shapes the full argument. Each section rewrite now shows its framework role and offers suitable angles (problem, outcome, hesitation or approach), alongside wording changes. Angles keep the existing page framework and CTA. PAS openings must recognize the supplied difficulty early; they may combine that recognition with a supported benefit. Heroes no longer inherit a mandatory outcome-led instruction that conflicts with PAS. Full stage instructions survive compact requests, and focused rewrites retain the target section, its neighbors, page headings and all approved facts.

A separate model-based editorial check reviews the actual copy for specificity, framework execution and unsupported promises, including confusion between program delivery and discovery-call outcomes. Findings must quote the affected section. One focused repair is allowed, followed by review of those changes. Exact approved testimony and unchanged sections remain protected. A failed or incomplete check, or an unresolved finding, becomes a visible review point and survives unrelated edits. This supports human editorial review; it does not certify persuasion or conversion performance. Claim repairs use small batches to stay inside request limits. Groq GPT-OSS uses its documented low reasoning setting, with a larger editorial response allowance so internal reasoning does not exhaust the JSON response.

For full generation, the optional editorial review and its repair share a 150-second allowance and stop by 270 seconds from generation start, leaving time to return completed copy before the API's five-minute deadline. Expiry aborts the editorial provider call, not the enclosing request. The last validated draft is returned with explicit incomplete-review flags or unresolved findings. Operator cancellation still cancels generation and leaves the saved project unchanged. Proof-framing word limits are stated in the writing instructions as well as checked on the result.

Public headings are generated from section purpose and approved offer details. Internal section names are excluded from model outlines; copy exports contain the generated headings without extra template-label headings. Approved proof cannot silently disappear from its section. Automatic review checks every approved full quotation with its corresponding author, flagging missing or abbreviated accounts and mismatched attribution even when other valid accounts remain. It also flags altered testimonial quotations, inconsistent CTA labels, em dashes outside quotations, common filler, generic hero invitations without an audience, invented financial stakes, unsupported follow-up arrangements and unconfirmed no-obligation or commitment promises (including Unicode hyphens), and unconfirmed call durations written in words. These checks support editorial review; they do not establish conversion performance or verify a client's claims independently.
