# Qualification and evidence

Finder → PostgreSQL queue/CRM → local LinkedIn capture → bounded public research → PostgreSQL result. See [engine-v2.md](engine-v2.md) for operation and configuration.

The main CRM qualification requires confirmed profile identity, followers strictly greater than `MIN_FOLLOWERS` (default 500), evidenced UK/US/Canada residence, an attributable email and reliably dated LinkedIn activity within 30 days. Version 3 adds the user-requested country gate and lowers the former 1,000-follower default. Business segment and commercial facts still rank/filter prospects independently. Missing followers, identity, country or activity remain REVIEW; they cannot be converted into rejection or qualification just to lower the review count. Existing research schema version 1 and qualification version 2 remain readable. Stored thresholds stay attached to historical evidence; a new/resumed check applies the current policy. See [finder-review-audit.md](finder-review-audit.md) for the stored-evidence regression and repair results.

Read follower evidence on the loaded profile immediately after identity. Store raw text, integer, applied threshold and timestamp. Keep connections separate. At/below threshold → REJECTED/FOLLOWERS_BELOW_MIN; absent/unparseable → REVIEW. Followers do not prove buying intent; this threshold is the requested screening rule. Qualification version is 3. Legacy qualified leads remain follower-unchecked without automatic requeue; explicitly selected follower rechecks refresh the profile and apply the current rule.

Case Study Hunter coach prospecting retains its existing independent-practice/offer criteria and optional email requirement. It also applies the follower rule. It does not inherit the main CRM's recent-activity or email gates. A missing/unusual headline can still be supported by About/website evidence after the follower check. Manual fit decisions cannot bypass an unknown/below-minimum follower count.

The worker skips remaining work after a decision. It checks activity before contact/website research when recent activity is required. Public research prioritizes home/contact/about links and stops once it has enough attributed evidence. Defaults: eight routes, twelve attempts, twenty wall-clock seconds, five seconds per request. Unreadable static HTML gets one bounded separate-browser attempt. Terminal site failures retain their evidence for REVIEW instead of a repeated crawl. Website/CRM retries reuse captured LinkedIn evidence.

Qualification remains separate from permission to send. SMTP verification, suppression, contact history, duplicate handling and account-specific messaging evidence retain their existing checks. No outreach starts as part of qualification. Replacing an email invalidates its old verification.

Private regression snapshots/replays are under ignored `data/engine-v2/`. The earlier performance rebuild's regression is documented in [engine-measurements.md](engine-measurements.md). A fresh 50-record set covers version 3; every decision delta, including the newly requested country policy and corrected uncertainty routing, is reported in [finder-review-audit.md](finder-review-audit.md). Root AGENTS.md lists the engine, CRM, type and lint checks.
